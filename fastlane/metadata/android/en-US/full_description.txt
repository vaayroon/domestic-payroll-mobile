Homeslip helps a private employer in Spain manage the payroll of household employees under the Special Regime for Household Employees (Régimen Especial de Empleados de Hogar).

What it does
- Computes the monthly net salary: gross salary minus the employee Social Security contribution.
- Prorates partial months on exact calendar days worked.
- Handles the June and December extra payments, either paid out or prorated.
- Generates a "Recibo Individual de Salarios" as a PDF.
- Sends the receipt through your mail app or the share sheet.
- Reminds you every month to run the payroll.
- Supports several employees.

Your data stays on your device
Homeslip works fully offline. It has no account, no backend, no analytics and no telemetry. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Unofficial app
Homeslip is an unofficial app and is not affiliated with the Seguridad Social or the TGSS. Always check the results against the official sources before relying on them.
